The Evolution

From broken Excel models to a global financial platform.

In 2020, our founding team had just closed a $4M seed round for our prior venture. Within six months, we discovered our outsourced bookkeeping firm was 90 days behind, missing critical burn spikes that nearly halved our true runway.

We realized that 82% of venture-backed failures aren't caused by bad product ideas—they occur because founders run out of cash before discovering product-market fit. We decided to solve this permanently.

Operating Principles

The Philosophy That Drives Our Work

We hold ourselves to the same uncompromising standards that top founders expect from their most critical engineering infrastructure.

Radical Transparency

No hidden assumptions, no hand-waving estimates. Every metric, chart, and runway projection links directly to verified source bank ledgers.

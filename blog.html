Quick Reference

The Founder's Financial Cheat Sheet

Instant clarity on the exact formulas and target benchmarks investors look for in board meetings.

Capital Efficiency

Burn Multiple

Net Burn divided by Net New ARR. Measures how much cash is burned to generate each $1 of new annual recurring revenue.

Target: < 1.2x (Excellent)
Retention

Net Dollar Retention

Starting ARR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn. Demonstrates organic expansion of existing client accounts.

Target: > 115% for SaaS
Acquisition

CAC Payback

Total sales and marketing spend divided by gross profit from new customers added. Time required to break even on acquisition.

Target: < 12 Months
Sales Velocity

Magic Number

Quarterly ARR growth annualized divided by prior quarter's sales & marketing expense. Measures efficiency of sales scaling.

Target: > 0.85x
